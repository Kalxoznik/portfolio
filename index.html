AI coding agents can write code fast, but left alone they lose context, repeat work and drift from the goal. Creative Space is a kanban board that turns them into a small team: a developer agent builds, an architect agent reviews and plans, and I stay in control as the product owner. Every task, handoff and decision is visible in one place.
